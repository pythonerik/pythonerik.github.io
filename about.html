About Erik

Story-first educator with a public service heart.

I believe that insurance is a promise—and that people deserve to understand the promises they make. I build human-centered education that empowers agencies and consumers to navigate risk with empathy and clarity.

What drives me

My path began in public service, where I learned how policy and compliance shape the lives of families and small businesses. Those years taught me the importance of precise, compassionate communication. Today, I bring that mindset into the insurance world, translating regulations into learning experiences that people can actually use.

Whether I am architecting a licensing curriculum, coaching new producers through their first policies, or designing interactive workshops, I focus on clarity. I partner with stakeholders to understand pain points, then craft journeys that make complex information feel approachable and actionable.

I stay curious by experimenting with new mediums—radio waves, market data, 3D visualizations—and by listening deeply to the communities I serve. Every project is a chance to build trust and give people the confidence to make informed decisions.

Guiding principles

  • Empathy first: Learning sticks when people feel seen, supported, and safe to ask questions.
  • Accuracy with context: Regulations matter, but so does the "why" behind them. I always pair requirements with real-world stories.
  • Iterate openly: Feedback from learners, regulators, and agency leaders helps me refine programs until they truly work.

Beyond the desk

Ham radio operations sharpen my problem-solving under pressure. Watching the markets teaches me how risk evolves in real time. And sculpting 3D art gives me a sandbox for visual storytelling. Together, these hobbies keep my mind nimble and my creativity flowing.
